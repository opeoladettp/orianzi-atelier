Where Haute Couture Meets Six Sigma Rigor.

With a Master of Science in Management Science from the University of Hertfordshire and executive operations leadership managing 700+ workforce networks for global conglomerates, Oluwakemi reimagines how luxury ateliers operate.

Orianzi Atelier integrates data-driven customer lifetime value (CVM) frameworks, Power BI analytics, and certified quality assurance governance directly into bespoke couture workflows. This ensures international patrons receive runway-grade garments with zero-defect tailoring and seamless cross-border delivery.
